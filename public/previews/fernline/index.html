The problem

A finished run is not a successful run.

Agents fail differently from APIs. They don't throw, they improvise. You need to see what they did, step by step, and stop the risky parts before they happen.

  1. 01

    Silent failures

    The run says “completed”. The agent looped on a tool, gave up and apologised to the customer. Nobody noticed for a week.

    1 in 9 “successful” runs has a hidden error

  2. 02

    Runaway costs

    A retry loop or a 200k-token context turns a $0.05 run into a $4 run. You find out on the invoice.

    Spend spikes are found 11 days late on average

  3. 03

    Unreviewed risky actions

    Refunds, emails to customers, SQL on production. Agents act with your credentials, and nobody signs off.

    63% of teams have no approval step for writes
