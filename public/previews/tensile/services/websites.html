Sites and apps

Tensile designs and builds marketing websites and web apps on Astro with a headless CMS such as Sanity. Every site we ship scores 100 on Lighthouse, is structured for search engines and AI assistants, and can be edited by your team without a developer.

Questions

Why Astro?

Astro ships plain HTML and almost no JavaScript, so pages load fast on any phone. It also works with every major headless CMS.

Can our team edit the site?

Yes. Every page is built from CMS content, and we run a one-hour handover session with your team before launch.

Do you migrate from WordPress or Webflow?

Yes. We move your content, keep your URLs or redirect them, and check rankings for a month after launch.
